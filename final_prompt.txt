**Role & Objective**
You are a senior software engineer tasked with fixing a regression bug in a Python shipping compliance engine, creating a fixed version of the project, and performing a complete validation of the finished solution.

```

├── src/
│   ├── __init__.py

│   ├── constraints.py
│   ├── calculator.py
│   ├── shipping_service.py
│   └── exceptions.py
├── tests/
│   ├── __init__.py

│   └── test_shipping_service.py
├── data/
│   └── sample_orders.json
├── requirements.txt
├── README.md
└── KNOWN_ISSUE.md
```

   - Review KNOWN_ISSUE.md to understand the bug analysis
